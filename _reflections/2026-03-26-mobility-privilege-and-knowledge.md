---
title: "Mobility, Privilege and the Making of Knowledge"
date: 2026-03-26
category: Field Notes
---

One of the main perks of being a researcher is that it's a job that can take you anywhere. In the course of a single year I've thought through the same questions in a Kuala Lumpur office, a Fribourg library, and a conference hotel somewhere between the two.

But the mobility that makes this possible is unevenly distributed in ways that shape what we end up knowing. The visa, the funding, the institutional affiliation that lets you walk into a seminar room on another continent, these are not neutral prerequisites for research. They decide whose questions get asked and whose answers get heard.

I want to use this space to think out loud about that, less as a critique and more as a set of field notes from the inside.
